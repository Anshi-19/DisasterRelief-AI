PURPOSE:
This source provides information about the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF),
National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF), State Disaster Mitigation Fund (SDMF),
and National Disaster Mitigation Fund (NDMF).

RELEVANCE:
The source states that SDRF is the primary fund available with State Governments
for responses to notified disasters and that SDRF is used for immediate relief.

The currently listed SDRF disaster categories include:
- Cyclone
- Earthquake
- Flood
among other notified disasters.

The source also explains that NDRF supplements SDRF in cases of disasters of
severe nature when adequate funds are not available in SDRF.
